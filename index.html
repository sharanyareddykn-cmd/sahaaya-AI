How SAHAAYA AI Works

From citizen registration to problem resolution.

STEP 01

Citizen Registration

Citizens create an account and receive a unique Citizen ID that can be used throughout the platform.

STEP 02

Report an Issue

Citizens submit a problem with its description, category, location, duration and affected population.

STEP 03

AI Analysis

The system analyzes the complaint and recommends category, severity, urgency and priority.

STEP 04

Human Action

Authorized NGOs or administrators verify the recommendation and update the complaint status.

AI-Assisted Decision Making

SAHAAYA AI does not simply collect complaints. It analyzes them and provides an explainable recommendation so that decision-makers can identify which problems require greater attention.

Responsible AI

SAHAAYA AI follows a human-in-the-loop approach. Artificial intelligence provides recommendations and explanations, while authorized humans remain responsible for verification, prioritization and final action.
